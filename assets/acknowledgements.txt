Integra Live was developed by the Integra project at Birmingham Conservatoire,
Birmingham City University, part-funded by the Culture Programme of the European
Union 2006-2011. This version is a port of its Flex user interface to JUCE.

It uses:

- libIntegra (GNU GPL v2) - the Integra server and module system
- libpd and Pure Data 0.43 (BSD licence) - audio processing
- Pd externals (bonk~, fiddle~, expr, freeverb~, partconv~ and others) and flext
- JUCE 8 (GNU AGPL v3 / commercial) - user interface and audio devices
- Lua 5.2 (MIT licence) - scripting
- libxml2, zlib, minizip (MIT / zlib licences)
- libsndfile (GNU LGPL), FFTW (GNU GPL), PortMidi (MIT-style licence),
  PortAudio (MIT licence), Boost (Boost licence), tmpfileplus (Mozilla Public
  License 2.0)
